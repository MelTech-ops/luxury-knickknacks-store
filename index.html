Who We Are

Hello, we're Luxury Knickknacks.

We build gift crates the way a good gift should feel: personal. Every order starts with a conversation about who it's for: their favorite characters, colors, and the occasion you're celebrating. From there, we design and hand-assemble a crate that's entirely theirs, not pulled from a shelf.

No templates and no mass production, just thoughtful, made-to-order gifting for birthdays, holidays, anniversaries, new babies, and everything worth celebrating.
